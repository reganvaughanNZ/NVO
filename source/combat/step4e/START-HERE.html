What this adds

Custom armour can share a reviewed coverage definition through an NVO authoring tag. An exact-record exception can give an unusual variant its own definition without changing the other suits. Contradictory tags and mistakes stop the export.

The new tool turns those choices into the format our existing DLL already reads. The shipped export is byte-for-byte identical to the file you just tested, so another game test would not exercise any changed runtime behaviour.

Are we adding KEYWORDS?

We are borrowing the classification concept and writing NVO's own authoring tool. This packet does not read live JIP/KEYWORDS tags or add the KEYWORDS loader. A future live adapter would be a separate integration decision.

Dependency and donor explanation · Authoring guide and limits

Custom armour workflow

  1. Import the real armour records and verify their owning plugin, local ID and equip mask.
  2. Assign a reviewed shared profile. Keep body armour and helmets independent.
  3. Add an explicit exception where a variant differs. Export and review the resulting exact mappings.

Power-armour labels alone grant no protection. Material strength, damage and stagger remain future work. A shared coverage definition does not mean two suits have identical materials or durability.
