What happens when tracking is full?

This temporary diagnostic fills tracking space with inert reservations. Your burst should make one shot use its original projectile, then restore normal capacity for later shots.

Damage replacement stays off. No GECK work or large battle needed.

  1. Launch through xNVSE. Load a disposable outdoor save with NVO.esm and NVOFlightPilot.esp active. Wait five seconds. AI may stay off. If needed:

    bat NVOFlightKit

  2. Equip the ordinary 9mm SMG with standard 9mm. Aim above the horizon into clear sky, away from nearby terrain/buildings, not straight overhead. Fire one short automatic burst, about five rounds. Do not fire separate slow taps. Exact count is unimportant.
  3. Wait ten seconds in gameplay. Reload the same save once and wait five seconds. Give the kit again if needed, then fire one standard 9mm pistol shot into the distance.
  4. Wait ten seconds in gameplay, quit normally, and reply Finished. Mention any new firing problem. No more shots or repeated test needed unless the log shows otherwise.

The test runs once per game launch and does not rearm on reload. The assistant will check the game-root NVOCombatCore.log.
