Packet 3B1: flight preview

Purpose: check the pilot profiles and speed inputs against live projectiles before changing flight. Expected plugin version: 301. No GECK work is needed.

The assistant installs the DLL, matching PDB and NVOFlightPreview.ini with a backup. Flight and damage remain unchanged. The installation receipt records completion separately.

Your short check

  1. Load a test save and wait three seconds.
  2. Outside VATS, fire two ordinary 9mm Pistol shots with standard 9mm, then two ordinary Hunting Rifle shots with standard .308, toward a wall or distant ground.
  3. Reload the save in the same process, wait three seconds and fire one more pilot shot.
  4. Exit and send NVOCombatCore.log beside FalloutNV.exe before launching again.

God mode is fine for this flight-input check. A log plus short weapon/ammo notes is sufficient.

GetPluginVersion "NVOCombatCore"
Optional test equipment commands

Only if you need the ordinary weapons/ammo. These add items when you enter them in the game console.

9mm Pistol: 

player.additem 000E3778 1

Standard 9mm (20): 

player.additem 0008ED03 20

Hunting Rifle: 

player.additem 00004333 1

Standard .308 (20): 

player.additem 0006B53C 20

Look for FLIGHT_PREVIEW_READY and matching FLIGHT_PREVIEW rows. Calculated donor previews are about 387.109 m/s and 849.771 m/s. Existing speed settings can differ. Hitscan/zero-time travel samples may report mean_speed_valid=0; that is expected when no usable travel measurement exists.
