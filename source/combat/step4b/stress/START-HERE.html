NVO 4B · Automatic-fire check

Uses the existing NVO 0.3.26 / native 326. Two console helper files are installed. No GECK work or DLL update is needed.

Purpose: check the armour reader while a humanoid carries 128 different junk items and takes repeated automatic fire. We will check its 64-snapshot limit and that it resumes after loading. Damage replacement remains off.

One short test

Use your disposable test save and usual AI-disabled setup, with a living human target. These helpers change test inventory and health. Do not overwrite your normal save. If god mode is on, turn it off so magazines empty normally.

  1. Get the SMG. Run:
    bat NVOStressKit4B
    This gives you a 9mm SMG and 300 standard rounds. Check that the 9mm SMG is equipped with standard 9mm. Reload its magazine fully.
  2. Prepare the target. Open the console, click the living human target and check its name appears at the top. Run this once:
    bat NVOStressTarget4B
    The target gets Combat Armor, a Combat Helmet, 128 distinct miscellaneous items and 100,000 health to survive the burst. Close the console, let the equipment appear, and make a separate manual save named NVO 4B Stress.
  3. Fire three full magazines. Stand a few metres away and aim at the torso. Empty three 30-round magazines into the same target, reloading the weapon between them. About 90 rounds total. Use ordinary aiming, without VATS. Notice any stuttering, freezing or unusual behaviour. Some misses are fine; keep the bursts on target so we can reach 65 or more landed hits.
  4. Reload once. Load NVO 4B Stress. Fire a short burst of roughly five rounds into the same target. Wait a few seconds and quit normally.

Reply “finished” and say whether it felt smooth or stuttered, whether the target survived, and if many shots missed. I will read the log directly. Please avoid relaunching before the log is captured.

What the result can tell us

Before the sample limit, the log should show complete armour scans over the enlarged inventory. After 64 snapshot attempts, detailed armour scanning stops and later hits increment omitted_after_limit. That count is expected in this test. After the save reload, the budget should reset and armour rows should return.

This checks bounded-reader stability and noticeable hitches. The current log has no per-scan timing measurements, so it cannot prove an exact frame-time cost or production performance with unlimited observation.
