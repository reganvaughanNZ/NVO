Load your test save and use the ordinary Hunting Rifle with standard .308. Keep the shot path clear of buildings and other obstructions.

  2. Normal hit: shoot a live target at a normal fighting distance outside VATS.
  3. Farther VATS hit: use VATS to hit another live target farther away.

Wait three seconds after each confirmed impact. If one misses, make at most one replacement shot for that check. After the final shot, wait 20 seconds, exit normally and tell me which shots hit or missed. No reload cycle, stress test or deliberate long miss is needed.
