Reserve before replacing

NVO now reserves physics and lifetime tracking space before replacing a projectile. If space is unavailable, the supplied projectile is kept.

Damage replacement remains off. No GECK edits. This check confirms the new admission path and reload cleanup.

  1. Launch through xNVSE. Load a disposable save with NVO.esm and NVOFlightPilot.esp active. Wait five seconds. AI may stay off.
  2. If needed, run the existing kit command:

    bat NVOFlightKit

    Use standard 9mm and .308 ammunition.
  3. Fire one 9mm pistol shot at a rock/building across open ground, roughly 50–100 metres away. An estimate is fine; the projectile needs some flight time.
  4. Fire one hunting-rifle VATS shot at a nearby living target. Then fire a short 9mm SMG burst manually at a distant rock/building.
  5. Reload the same save once. Wait five seconds, give the kit again if needed, and fire one 9mm pistol shot across open ground.
  6. Wait a few seconds, quit normally, and reply Finished. Mention any new firing problem or crash. Missed shots do not require a repeat.

The log is NVOCombatCore.log in the game folder. No large NPC stress battle is needed for this check.
