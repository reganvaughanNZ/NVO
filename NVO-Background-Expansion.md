# NVO background expansion proposals

Status: design proposals requested by the user, not implemented gameplay. Based on the user's 58 background descriptions, the existing donor scripts and NVO_Intended_Changes.txt. The scope document supplies direction; its proposed simulation systems are not assumed to exist in the current ESM.

## What a background should provide

Each origin should provide a working equipment package, a signature activity, a useful personal relationship, an opening opportunity and a lasting complication. A profession should still matter after its starting weapon has been replaced.

NVO is intended for hardcore play balanced around a single life. Distinguish hard background restrictions from recoverable hardships. Exile and outlaw status are hard restrictions: the character can become more capable and build a life around those restrictions, but is not promised reinstatement, a pardon or a reputation reset. Poverty, damaged equipment and lack of training are examples of conditions that can improve through relevant play. Classify each drawback individually before implementation; neither universal reversibility nor universal permanence is the rule.

Permadeath is a design assumption, not an enforced save policy. Do not delete saves, disable loading, corrupt saves or otherwise prevent the player from continuing by their own choice. Do not add save or death enforcement scripts. The balance must take account of survival, uncertainty, loss and limited opportunities without relying on repeated reloads as the intended solution.

Struggling, established or veteran variants are appropriate only where they fit the background. An easier equipment variant must not silently erase the defining history or handicap. Origins need not offer equivalent power or equal access to the world.

A kit should account deliberately for weapons, ammunition, clothing or armour, tools, provisions, medicine and identity material. A desperate background may intentionally lack several of these. Exact quantities, omissions and condition must support its intended challenge and be disclosed where they define the start. We must reconcile these kits with ALTStartingGearUDF so generic tagged-skill grants do not accidentally duplicate equipment or erase deprivation.

## Restrictions and single-life balance

- Hard restrictions should create a distinct way of living. Brotherhood exile continues to exclude reinstatement and normal Brotherhood membership benefits. An outlaw remains wanted by the relevant authorities. Individual allies, covert help, alternate suppliers or evasion can make survival possible without erasing that status. A personal friendship is not faction-wide forgiveness.
- Recoverable hardships need relevant means of improvement: earn money, repair gear, receive appropriate treatment or obtain training. A drawback's exact permanence and recovery rules must be decided for that background, including addictions or physical conditions. Blanket item bans need a concrete training, physical or other relevant reason.
- Major starting conditions, permanent restrictions and unusually dangerous openings must be explained before selection. Distinguish a permanent restriction from a recoverable hardship in the preview. Ordinary exploration can remain uncertain; do not require undisclosed scripted death or trial-and-error reloads to learn the only viable opening action.
- Powerful origins may begin with better protection, allies or supplies. Their obligations and vulnerabilities should be concrete, and they remain mortal. Do not force every advantage to carry an arbitrary matching penalty.
- Followers and contacts can die. Their loss can end a service, income source or quest. Failure should produce a valid world state rather than an impossible objective or broken script; replacement access is not guaranteed.
- Injury, addiction, scarcity, faction exclusion and employment obligations should shape decisions beyond character creation. Record each drawback's classification, affected services or relationships, available survival strategies, and any applicable recovery route with its effort and costs. Do not invent redemption quests or generic payments to remove hard exile or outlaw restrictions.
- Donor conditions described as incurable, such as Nuka-Cola addiction and radio-dial compulsion, require an explicit individual design decision. The latest direction does not automatically make all conditions curable. None of these mechanics has yet been changed in the ESM by this design work.
- When a recoverable hardship is resolved, the promised mechanical improvement must occur. Hard status remains separately recorded; earning caps, ordinary reputation gains or a story-wide pardon must not accidentally clear an intended permanent background restriction. Review such interactions during implementation.
- Consequences of later player choices, failed quests and deaths remain meaningful. Overcoming background restrictions does not promise resurrection or reversal of every event during a run.
- Retreat, surrender, preparation and abandoning an objective should have value where the situation supports them. These are possible actions, not guarantees of survival or escape.
- Becoming the Courier remains optional. This does not promise that every hostile origin can freely access every flier or complete every main-quest path.

The tables describe proposed authored additions. New NPC contacts, followers, service access and regional activities require implementation. They are not claims about existing donor content or established Fallout canon.

## Systems that can be shared

- Work orders: commissioned task, issuer, requirements, objective, payment, deadline where useful and outcome. First use small authored jobs; integrate regional needs after that NVO module exists.
- Personal contacts: track concrete favours, reliability and witnessed misconduct. Faction reputation and one person's trust are separate values.
- Followers: design an identity, recruitment condition, role, limits, departure conditions and consequences of death. Keep ED-E and Dogmeat integrated with their existing systems. Avoid granting a permanent follower to every background. A loss need not have an automatic replacement.
- Supplies and ownership: distinguish gifts, loans, stolen items, wages and player property. Role-specific access should cover designated items and locations, not an entire faction inventory.
- Occupation progression: reward completed jobs or discoveries with recipes, services, introductions and useful equipment. Do not make unrelated people instantly know about a private action.
- Efficient state: use explicit objectives and relevant events, finite discovery markers and infrequent business updates. The wider economy, injury, disguise, morale and witness systems are planned dependencies, not features that this document implements.

## Recommended first implementation

Prototype Prospector as a complete profession before duplicating the pattern. Proposed starting kit: a serviceable varmint rifle with a modest ammunition supply, shovel, work clothes, repair kit, a few bobby pins, water, food, two basic healing items, limited caps and a salvage notebook. Final item IDs and counts must be resolved against the current NVO records before giving GECK code.

Its first playable addition should be one authored salvage assignment with a small number of nearby candidate sites and one buyer. Let the player choose whether to sell a recovered component, use it to repair their equipment or fulfil the buyer's order. Mark each site and payment as completed once. Add the proposed missing partner and recruitment only after the core occupation works.

Build three contrasting backgrounds in depth first: Prospector for occupation and survival supplies, Brotherhood Exile for a hard institutional restriction alongside valuable expertise, and NCRCF Convict for outlaw life with alternate contacts and trade. Keep the remainder on their donor behaviour until their own upgrade turn. Equipment is the first implementation step, followed by one usable activity, then a contact and consequences. Do not begin by adding 58 large quest chains or a universal forgiveness system.
